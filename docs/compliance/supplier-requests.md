# Supplier document requests

**DRAFT emails, not sent.** Prepared 3 November 2026 for the business owner. Fill in the blanks and send from the company address. Keep what comes back outside the public repository, and record it in `external-actions.md`. Receiving these documents does not make the finished watch compliant: they are evidence for the assessment of the final product, which has its own battery, housing and firmware.

## 1. Waveshare (the board)

**Subject:** Compliance documents for ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06

Hello,

We are ______ (company number ______), based in the United Kingdom. We plan to fit your ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06 board into a finished consumer watch, with our own firmware and battery, and to sell it in Great Britain.

Quantity expected: ______. Board revision we hold: ______.

For our technical file, please send the following for this exact board and revision, or tell us if a document does not exist:

1. Radio test reports (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, EN 300 328 or equivalent), with the laboratory's name and test date.
2. EMC test reports (EN 301 489 or equivalent).
3. Electrical safety and RF exposure reports (EN 62368-1, EN 62311 or equivalent).
4. Any assessment of the cybersecurity requirements of the Radio Equipment Directive (EN 18031).
5. Your CE and/or UKCA Declaration of Conformity, with the standards it applies.
6. RoHS and REACH declarations, with test reports if available.
7. For the radio module and antenna used: module certificates, antenna type and gain, and any conditions on using the module in a finished product.
8. Information on the audio, display and power-management components, if it affects the reports above.
9. Any changes between revisions, and how you tell customers about future changes.

Please also confirm:

- Can these reports be relied on for a finished product that adds a battery and a housing, and if so on what conditions?
- Do you permit us to include copies in our technical file and show them to authorities?
- Who is the legal manufacturer named on these documents, and at what address?

Thank you,

______

## 2. EEMB (the battery)

**Subject:** Compliance documents for LP402535 battery

Hello,

We are ______ (company number ______), based in the United Kingdom. We plan to use your LP402535 3.7 V 320 mAh lithium polymer battery in a consumer wearable sold in Great Britain, and we will ship it inside the product.

Quantity expected: ______. Exact part number and any protection circuit option: ______.

Please send the following for this exact part:

1. Datasheet and specification sheet: capacity, voltage limits, charge and discharge rates, temperature ranges, dimensions, cycle life.
2. UN 38.3 test report and test summary.
3. IEC 62133-2 test report, with the laboratory's name and test date.
4. Safety Data Sheet.
5. Transport documents for shipping cells inside equipment by road, air and post (UN 3481).
6. Description of the protection circuit, and what it guards against.
7. RoHS and REACH declarations.
8. Declared content for battery regulations and labelling: chemistry, and any mercury, cadmium or lead.
9. Date code and batch traceability, and how you tell customers about changes.

Please also confirm:

- That these reports cover the exact model, and any limits on using it in a wearable worn against the skin.
- Your recommended charging voltage and current, and storage conditions.
- The legal manufacturer's name and address.

Thank you,

______
