Get started in about 5 minutes

  1. Charge and turn on. Hold the bottom button for about a second. A new watch opens on its Wi-Fi setup screen.
  2. Open your olá account on your phone at app.olacompanion.com (scan the code) and sign up or sign in.
  3. Connect the watch to Wi-Fi. Android: tap “Connect to watch” in Chrome and type the password the watch shows. iPhone: scan the code on the watch with the Camera, choose your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) and tap Save.
  4. Pair. The watch restarts and shows a 6-digit code. Enter it in your account under Add watch within 5 minutes.
  5. Say hello. Press the top button, or tap the big mic, and just speak.

Using olá

Talk

Tap the big mic or press the top button and speak. No wake word.

Interrupt

Tap again while olá is speaking to cut in.

Dialog mode

Hold the mic for 2 seconds: the mic reopens after every reply. A tap ends it.

Wake and power

Press the bottom button to wake the screen. Hold it for 2 seconds to turn off.

Make it yours

Tap the gear for theme, brightness, volume and language. Persona, voice and history are in your olá account.

olácare subscription

Your first 30 days are free, starting when you pair the watch. Then £7.99 a month, renewing monthly: unlimited AI conversations, history, personas and updates. Cancel anytime in your account (Account › Manage billing). Without an active subscription the assistant does not answer; your notes and reminders stay in your account.

Your rights & warranty

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 your watch must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If it isn't:

30 days

Reject it for a full refund.

6 months

We repair or replace it. If that fails, a refund or price reduction.

6 years

You can claim for up to 6 years (5 in Scotland).

How to claim: email ola@olacompanion.com with your order number and a short description. We cover the cost of returning a faulty watch. Not covered: normal wear and tear, accidental damage, misuse or unauthorised repair. We do not offer an extra commercial warranty; nothing here limits your legal rights.

Changed your mind? You can cancel within 14 days after the day you receive the watch, without giving a reason: email us with your order number, then send the watch back within 14 days. Unless it is faulty, you pay the cost of returning it. Cancelling the watch also cancels olácare.

Security updates are provided for at least 2 years from the date you buy the watch. Full terms: olacompanion.com/legal

Battery and charging

Contains a lithium-polymer battery. Do not open, puncture, crush or heat the watch, or leave it in direct sun or a hot car. Charge only with the supplied cable and a compliant USB power adapter. Stop using it if it swells, gets hot or smells.
